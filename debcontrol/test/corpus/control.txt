Simple source-only control file
================================================================================

Maintainer: John Doe <john@example.com>
Section: devel
Priority: optional

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  (blank_line)
  (stanza
    (field (field_name) (field_value))
    (field (field_name) (field_value))
    (field (field_name) (field_value))
    (field (field_name) (field_value))))
